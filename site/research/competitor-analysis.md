### Baseline

The page this market already has opens with a headline and one CTA over a product UI demo or video, on a dark or black ground, set in a single proprietary brand sans, illustrated with product UI screenshots and site mockups, moving through an autoplay video or animated demo, selling a prompt-to-site or app builder that also hosts the result, proving itself with a strip of company logos, priced as a free tier plus a paid monthly plan, written in reassuring plain language for non-technical owners, and giving the visitor nothing to take away before sign-up.

### Gaps

- No peer opens with a buyer's own words (a quoted complaint or question) as the headline.
- No peer shows its output next to a generic AI-made page (a before/after or side-by-side comparison).
- No peer shows a quality gate as imagery: a build report, failing checks or speed and accessibility scores.
- No peer offers measured page quality (load speed, Lighthouse or accessibility scores) of pages people shipped as proof.
- No peer's tool researches real buyers before it designs; customer research appears only as a book to read (P019).
- No peer delivers one static folder to upload anywhere with no account, platform or runtime; every builder hosts the result, the boilerplate needs Next.js.
- No peer promises to refuse or block a page that fails its checks.
- No peer gives a plain-language request to paste into the visitor's own coding agent; the only takeaways for agents are shell commands (P010, P013) or a skill file (P012).
- No peer uses serif body text; serif appears only as display type (P005, P009).
