The problem

Your team agreed on something. It's somewhere in reply 83.

Slack is great for talking things through. It is terrible at remembering what you landed on. So people scroll, re-ask, or miss the call entirely.

Scroll

Anyone who was offline has to read the whole thread to find the one line that matters to them.

Re-ask

"Wait, what did we decide?" restarts a conversation that was already finished.

Drop

To-dos mentioned in passing never get an owner, so they quietly never happen.

How it works

Three steps, all inside Slack.

Nothing to learn and no dashboard to check. Threadly lives where the conversation already is.

Add Threadly to Slack

Install it to your workspace in about a minute and invite it to the channels you want covered.

Ask for a summary

Type /threadly in any thread, or let it auto-summarize threads that pass a reply count you choose.

Get decisions and to-dos

A short summary is posted in the thread: decisions, to-dos with owners and dates, and anything still unresolved.

Features

Built for teams that talk a lot and read a little.

Every summary has the same shape, so people learn where to look.

Decisions, not recaps

Threadly separates what was agreed from what was only discussed, so the summary is short and you can act on it.

To-dos with owners

Action items are attributed to the person who took them on, with due dates when someone mentioned one.

Every line links to its source

Click any decision to jump to the exact message it came from. Easy to check, easy to trust.

Open questions flagged

Unresolved points are called out so the thread ends with a clear next step instead of fading out.

Auto-summarize long threads

Set a threshold per channel, like 30 replies, and Threadly steps in without anyone asking.

Only where you invite it

Threadly only reads channels it has been added to. Remove it from a channel and it stops reading that channel.

FAQ

Questions, answered briefly.

How does the free trial work?

You get the full product for 14 days. When the trial ends you choose whether to continue at $12 per user per month. If you don't, Threadly simply stops summarizing.

Who counts as a user?

Anyone on your Threadly plan who can request or receive summaries. You decide who is on it, and you can change that at any time.

Can Threadly see all my Slack messages?

No. It only reads channels it has been invited to, and only when summarizing. Remove it from a channel and it stops reading there.

What if a summary gets something wrong?

Every line links to the message it came from, so checking takes a click. You can also edit or delete a summary right in the thread.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no annual contracts. Cancel from your workspace settings and you won't be billed again.
