# lfm on the page an agent wrote from one sentence

The agent got this request, with no research, no skills and no lfm:

> Build me a landing page for my new SaaS: Threadly, an app that summarizes long Slack threads into decisions and to-dos for busy teams. Pricing is $12 per user per month with a 14-day free trial.

Its file, default-agent-page.html, became the template of a fresh `lfm init` project whose lfm.json carries the page's own title and description. The five template.unused warnings come from that wrapping, not from the page, and are left out of the counts on lfm.cash.
